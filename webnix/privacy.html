Webnix privacy policy

Webnix is developed and published by ARUL LOGIC (ARUL G), Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Webnix ("the app") is a web browser with built-in developer tools for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary

Webnix does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Browsing data: history, bookmarks, open tabs and settings are stored locally in the app.
  • Developer data: snippets, request override rules and DevTools preferences are stored locally.
  • Website data: websites you visit may store cookies, local storage, IndexedDB and cached files inside the app's web view, as in any browser.

You can delete this data at any time in Settings › Clear browsing data and Settings › Storage & cache, or by uninstalling the app.

Network access

The app connects only to:

  • the websites you choose to open, and
  • those same websites, when you use DevTools features that fetch data (for example View page source, image previews, site audits or request overrides).

Text typed into the address bar that is not a web address is sent as a search to the search engine you select in Settings (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Startpage). Their own privacy policies apply to those searches.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a crash report (technical information such as the error and device model) is saved on your device. It is never sent automatically. It leaves your device only if you tap Share report and choose an app to send it with.

Exports and sharing

Screenshots, HAR files, bug reports, page source and audit/performance reports are created only when you request them, and are shared only through the Android share sheet to the app you pick. Temporary export files are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up the app's settings, bookmarks, history, snippets and override rules. Cookies and website data are never included in backups.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load web pages and to show an offline notice.
  • Files are picked through the system file picker only when a website asks you to upload a file; the app does not get general access to your storage.
  • Downloads are handed to Android's Download Manager.

Children

Webnix is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and in the app.
