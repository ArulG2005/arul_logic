Sawcut privacy policy

Sawcut Construction Calculator is developed and published by ARUL LOGIC (ARUL G), Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Sawcut ("the app") is a feet-inch and construction calculator for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary

Sawcut does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

  • Calculator data: calculation history, saved jobs, the last values you entered and your settings (region, units, language, precision) are stored only inside the app on your device.
  • Pro unlock: after you buy Sawcut Pro, the app stores an unlock code on your device. The code is made from a one-way hash of your device's Android ID and the app's package name, so it only works on that device. The Android ID itself is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • PDF reports: PDF reports you create are saved temporarily in the app's private cache. The app keeps only the 5 most recent reports.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Purchases

Sawcut Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Sawcut never sees your payment details. The app asks the Google Play app on your device only whether your Google account owns Sawcut Pro, and checks Google's signature on that purchase. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy.

Install check

To protect against pirated copies, the app checks on your device which app store installed it. This information stays on your device.

Backup, restore and sharing

  • Back up / Restore in the app write or read a file that you choose with the system file picker. The app does not get general access to your storage.
  • Share, Copy and PDF send results only to the app you pick through the Android share sheet or clipboard.
  • If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. ARUL LOGIC never sees it.

Permissions

Sawcut requests no dangerous permissions and no Internet permission. The Google Play billing permission is used only to talk to the Google Play app for purchases.

Children

Sawcut is a general-audience tool for construction and DIY work. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
